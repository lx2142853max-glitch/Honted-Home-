Keeper’s Journal

The Three Seals

  • Symbol A — Soul Spiral. Three curved lines around a central dot. The seal of Vorak, keeper of the Spirit Archive in the East Wing. It governs spirit, memory and passage.
  • Symbol B — Marionette Knot. A small diamond with four thread-like arms. The seal of Morvex, keeper of the Puppet Workshop in the West Wing. It governs control, connection and repetition.
  • Symbol C — Broken Crown. Three upward points with a gap in the centre. The seal of Grimthor, keeper of the Forbidden Study and the Tower. It governs authority — and curse.
  • Symbol D — House Crest. A tower inside a circle with three outer marks. Set into the front gate. The estate itself.

Fragments from the mansion

Goal

Recover the three Seals. Bear them to the Tower Sanctum. Then descend to the Null Chamber and learn what the fourth pedestal holds.

All supernatural events in this chapter are fictional game mechanics. A quiet house is not necessarily an empty one.

How to Play

Controls (Desktop)

  • WASD or arrow keys — walk
  • Shift — run  ·  C — crouch (quieter)
  • Mouse — look around (360°)  ·  E or Click — interact / open doors
  • J — journal  ·  Esc — release cursor

Controls (Touch / Android)

  • Left thumb — virtual joystick to walk. Right thumb — drag to look.
  • On-screen Interact button opens doors and uses objects. Journal opens the journal.

The Estate

  • Grounds: outer forest → iron gate → long stone approach → front garden → mansion.
  • House: 3 full floors, an attic, a central tower, a basement with crypt and tunnels.
  • Find the three Seals: East Wing (Séance Room / Archive), West Wing (Doll Workshop), Attic (Forbidden Study).
  • With three Seals, the tower door opens. From the Sanctum the way down to the Null Chamber is revealed.

Reading the symbols

Symbols carved into stone doors, floor tiles and notes are gameplay locks, not decoration. If a symbol looks important, it is. The gate crest on the front iron gate carries three empty recesses — the mansion will remind you of them.

The Fourth Pedestal

The Unknown Mark

The three Seals turn as one. Somewhere beneath the storm, something older than the mansion loosens — an incomplete circle with a missing centre, pressed into stone no hand has touched for three hundred years.
